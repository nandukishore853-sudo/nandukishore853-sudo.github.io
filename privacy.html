Privacy Policy

Colour Birds (package: com.max.flappytrumpp)
Effective date: November 8, 2026

This policy explains what information the Colour Birds app (“the app”) uses. We keep it simple: the app does not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address or phone number.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress on your device only. This includes your high scores, coins, unlocked birds and backgrounds, upgrades, and settings such as sound and vibration. This data is not sent to us. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Advertising

The app shows ads using Google AdMob, for example an optional video you can watch to revive in the game. AdMob, which is provided by Google, may collect and use information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data to show ads, measure performance, prevent fraud and, where allowed, personalise ads.

  • You can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation in your device’s Settings (usually under Google or Privacy).
  • Where required, for example in the European Economic Area and the UK, you may be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.
  • Learn more: How Google uses data from ads and Google Privacy Policy.

Information we do not collect

  • No account, name, email or phone number
  • No precise location
  • No contacts, photos, microphone or camera access

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. The only third party that receives data through the app is Google, through its advertising services, as described above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page with a new effective date.
